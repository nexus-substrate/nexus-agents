The corroboration check on the two live untrusted-input paths (`issue_triage`, `pr_review`) now runs inside the shared `HostileInputFirewall`, through its `corroboration` stage and `validateAction`, instead of being composed beside it by each caller (#6309, closing the last child of #5281). The shared instance enables the stage, and the result is narrowed on `evaluated`: a stage that did not run fails the call closed, the same way an unevaluated policy stage does, rather than being read as satisfied. Under `NEXUS_FIREWALL_POLICY=off` (the default) the verdicts are unchanged — `corroborated: false` on the triage record, `INSUFFICIENT_CORROBORATION` on the review-posting verdict. Under `audit` the verdicts are the same and a would-be refusal is logged with the missing requirements, so a soak can measure the refusal rate. Under `enforce` an uncorroborated action is refused by the firewall (`POLICY_REFUSED` at stage `corroboration`) and not executed; it is recorded as refused rather than dropped — it stays on the triage's typed action list with `policyApproved: false`, `refusedAtStage: 'corroboration'` and the missing requirements named, renders like a policy refusal, and the review post is blocked with the same reason. Additive API: `FirewallError.missing` carries the unmet requirements on a corroboration refusal, the evaluated `ActionValidation` carries `clearedOnlyByUnverifiedSources`, and `validateActionCorroboration` / `FirewallCorroborationDecision` are exported from `dogfooding/untrusted-input-firewall`.
